The 2026 PMP exam it's built for

PMI changed the PMP exam on July 9, 2026. The exam has 180 questions (170 scored and 10 unscored pretest questions) in 240 minutes, with two 10-minute breaks. The domain weights changed to People 33%, Process 41% and Business Environment 26%. Business Environment went from 8% to 26%. Case study questions, where several questions share one project scenario, are part of the new exam.

Source: PMI's 2026 PMP exam page

FAQ

Is PM Mastery built for the 2026 PMP exam?

Yes. PM Mastery is the PMP exam simulator built for the 2026 exam. Its 4,000+ questions, including 200 in 40 case studies, follow the 2026 Exam Content Outline, and its mock exams use the 180-question, 240-minute format.

Does PM Mastery give me the 35 contact hours?

No. PM Mastery is exam practice. You need the 35 contact hours (or an active CAPM) from another provider before you apply.

How much does PM Mastery cost?

It's free to start with 100 practice questions. Paid options are the Exam Prep Pass at $99 one time for 90 days, Premium at $49 per month, or Lifetime at $399.

How do I know if I'm ready for the PMP?

Take the free 10-question readiness check, then use the readiness score and domain breakdown as you practice. Know if you're ready before exam day.

Is there a refund?

Yes. Every paid plan has a 7-day money-back guarantee on your first purchase. One refund per customer.

Who makes PM Mastery?

PM Mastery is made by PM Mastery Solutions, LLC in New Orleans, Louisiana, founded by George (Ben) Schenck Jr.
