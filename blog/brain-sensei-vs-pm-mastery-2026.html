Settle the Contact Hours Question First

PMI requires 35 contact hours of project management education before you can apply for the PMP. Brain Sensei's course covers them. PM Mastery doesn't, and none of the time you spend in PM Mastery counts toward them.

So if you still need your hours, the comparison stops here: you need a course, whether that's Brain Sensei or another provider. If your hours are already covered by a Udemy course, employer training or a college class, you're really choosing how to spend the rest of your prep time and budget.

Question Banks and Full-Length Exams

Brain Sensei lists 2,200+ questions, three fixed 180-question practice exams, and an unlimited exam that builds a new test from the bank each time. PM Mastery has 4,000+ questions, including 200 in 40 case studies written for the 2026 linked-scenario format, and unlimited 180-question mocks on any paid plan, weighted to the 2026 split of People 33%, Process 41% and Business Environment 26%.

Most candidates never answer every question either platform has. Bank size matters most after your first thousand or so: a bigger pool means your next mock still shows you questions you haven't seen, which counts if you're studying for more than three months or retaking the exam.

Both give you repeated full-length practice. The PMP is 180 questions in 240 minutes, and sitting more than one full timed run is stamina training, not just a knowledge check. For more on the format, see how the 2026 case study questions work.

How Each One Teaches

Brain Sensei teaches up front. The animated samurai story and the course modules are built to make the material stick the first time you see it, which helps if a straight video lecture loses you after twenty minutes.

PM Mastery teaches after you miss. Every question has a written explanation of why the right answer is right and why the others are wrong. The AI Coach, powered by Claude, takes follow-up questions on the concept or the trap in a question: 3 a day on the free plan, 25 a day on paid plans.

If you learn a framework best from a story, Brain Sensei fits. If you learn by getting questions wrong and digging into why, PM Mastery fits.

Finding Your Weak Spots

PM Mastery gives you a readiness score and a diagnostic that ranks your weakest domains and tasks, so you know what to drill next instead of staring at one overall percentage. The 10-question readiness check at the top of this page is free and needs no signup.

Score breakdowns vary a lot between platforms, so check what Brain Sensei's trial shows you before you buy if that readout matters to you.

Which One to Pick

Pick Brain Sensei if you still need your 35 contact hours, you want one course that takes you from zero to exam-ready, and a story keeps you engaged better than lectures do.

Pick PM Mastery if your hours are covered, you learn by practicing and studying your mistakes, and you want unlimited 2026-format mocks and a clear readout of your weak domains. You can start free.

Use both if you have the time: Brain Sensei first for the framework and the hours, then PM Mastery for the last 8 to 12 weeks of practice. Not sure you qualify yet? Check the 2026 PMP requirements first.

Common Questions

Is Brain Sensei or PM Mastery better for the 2026 PMP exam?
Neither is better at everything, because they do different jobs. Brain Sensei is a course that gives you the 35 contact hours PMI requires to apply. PM Mastery is a practice platform with 4,000+ questions, including 200 in 40 case studies, and it doesn't provide contact hours.

How much does Brain Sensei cost compared to PM Mastery?
Brain Sensei lists $199 a year or $59 a month, and in November 2026 it was on sale at $89 a year and $29 a month. PM Mastery is free for 100 questions, then $99 for a 90-day Exam Prep Pass, $49 a month, or $399 lifetime.

Does PM Mastery give you the 35 contact hours for the PMP application?
No. PM Mastery is exam practice, not a contact-hours course. You need a separate 35-hour course, such as Brain Sensei or a Udemy course, to apply for the PMP.

Can I use Brain Sensei and PM Mastery together?
Yes. A common split is Brain Sensei first for the framework and the contact hours, then PM Mastery in the final 8 to 12 weeks for practice volume, full mock exams, and a read on your weak domains.
